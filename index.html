About Me

"Motivation Is Fleeting Like Emotions. Discipline Is Key."

I've been athletic my whole life — endurance and cardio first, then a full shift into resistance training, where I found what I actually wanted to spend my life doing: understanding what the body is truly capable of, and helping other people find out for themselves.

I'm certified through NCI as a Nutrition Coach and through NASM as a Personal Trainer. I bring both disciplines together because neither works as well without the other.

Life gets busy and time moves fast when you're grinding through your daily list. What I've learned is that motivation isn't a reliable foundation — it comes and goes like any emotion. The real shift happens when you stop relying on how you feel and start building structure through discipline and habit. Neuroplasticity is real: repeat the right behaviors consistently and they stop feeling like effort and start feeling like you. That's the goal.

Every program I write is built from scratch — your ability, your schedule, your health history, your goals. No templates, no copy-paste. That's what 1-on-1 actually means.
